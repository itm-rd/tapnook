Tapnook

Five calm puzzles in one small app: Arrows, Sort, Connect, Sudoku and Merge. Free, offline, no login.

Get it on Google Play

Arrows

Tap an arrow and it slides off the board if nothing is in its way. Clear them all to win.

Sort

Pour colors from tube to tube until every tube holds one color.

Connect

Join each pair of dots with a line and fill the whole board.

Sudoku

The classic number puzzle, with notes and hints. Every board has exactly one answer.

Merge

Swipe, and equal numbers join. Reach 2048 and beat your best score.

Daily puzzle

One larger arrow puzzle every day. Keep your streak and win a trophy for each full month.
