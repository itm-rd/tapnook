Tapnook – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

Tapnook ("the app") is a free puzzle game for Android with five games: Arrows, Sort, Connect, Sudoku and Merge. This policy explains what information is used when you play. There is no account and no login.

1. Information stored on your phone

The app remembers your progress (levels, best scores, a game in progress, hints, daily puzzles, themes), your settings (sound, vibration) and a few counters (for example, so ads are not shown too often). This stays on your phone. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. The developer cannot see it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interactions (views and clicks) and diagnostics.

Google uses this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message. They can change their choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options inside the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

3. Online settings

The app may download a small public settings file (for example, how often ads may appear) from this website. No personal information is sent, apart from the normal technical data any web request includes (such as IP address).

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and the settings file. The games themselves work offline.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or call logs.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. For questions, contact the developer through the email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
